Background screening, only with the person's say-so

look checks official public records about someone for a job, a lease, or a licence, after that person has read exactly what will be searched and signed to agree.

Open the app

Consent first

Nothing runs until the person signs through their own private link. Saying no deletes their details.

Official records only

Sanctions lists, company registries and licensing boards. No social media, no people-search sites.

No tracking data

Never collects home addresses, phone numbers, family members, or location history.

Your report, your rights

The person sees their report and who checked them and why, can dispute mistakes, and can have their data erased.

How it works

  1. An organisation asks for a check and says why it is needed.
  2. You get a link showing exactly which records will be searched. You sign, or decline.
  3. The check runs against official public records. Anything found is a possible match until a person reviews it.
  4. You get your own copy of the report, and can dispute anything that is wrong.
Follows GDPR rules for EU checks: a lawful basis is required, criminal-record checks need a law that authorises them, and reports are deleted after the retention period.
